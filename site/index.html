01How it works

DJI's own app sends wireless transfers to a phone only; to get files onto a computer it's a cable or a card reader. Osmotic lets the Mac talk to the camera directly.

  1. Bluetooth

    The Mac finds the camera and pairs with it. You approve once, on the camera.

  2. Wi-Fi

    The camera hands over its Wi-Fi password and the Mac joins its network.

  3. Download

    New files land in a folder per day, dated when they were shot.

  4. Back home

    When you disconnect, the camera returns to normal and your Mac rejoins your Wi-Fi.

02What it does

Tab: Files

The card, on your Mac

Thumbnails by day, with filters for videos, photos, starred and new. Download New takes everything that isn't on your Mac yet. A clip already there is skipped, never replaced, and a download that stops halfway picks up where it left off.

Tab: Live

Control it from across the room

Start and stop recording, take photos, switch between Video, Photo, Slow-mo and Low Light, and watch the camera's picture live over Wi-Fi. Back in Files, what you just shot is already listed.

Tab: Webcam

A webcam with a gimbal

Plug the camera in with USB-C and choose Webcam on it. The picture shows up in Osmotic, and in Zoom, Meet, FaceTime or OBS even with Osmotic closed. Not yet tested on hardware.

03The details

  • Dated right

    Each file gets the moment it was shot as its date, so Finder and your editor sort it correctly.

  • Light previews

    Space bar opens a preview from the camera's small .LRF proxy, not the full 4K file.

  • The extras

    RAW .DNG next to photos and the backup .WAV next to clips. On by default; turn them off in Settings.

  • Keyboard first

    Arrows to move, ⇧ to extend, space to preview, ⌘D and ⇧⌘D to download.

  • Updates itself

    New versions come from GitHub Releases and install only if their signature checks out.

  • Private

    No account and no analytics. Nothing leaves your Mac except the update check with GitHub.

05Install

  1. 01

    Download the .dmg, open it and drag Osmotic onto Applications.

  2. 02

    Open it. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the app because this build isn't notarized by Apple yet. Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

  3. 03

    Turn on the camera and click Connect. The first time, approve the pairing on the camera's screen.

07Questions

Is this made by DJI?

No. It's an independent open-source project, not affiliated with DJI. It never deletes or changes files already on the camera.

Do I still need DJI Mimo?

Only to activate a brand-new camera, which DJI does through Mimo. After that, Osmotic doesn't need your phone.

Why does my Mac lose Internet while connected?

The Mac joins the camera's own Wi-Fi, which has no Internet. Ethernet, or an iPhone sharing its connection over a cable, keeps you online. When you disconnect, your Mac rejoins your network.

How fast is it?

About 33 MB/s with a Pocket 3 in our tests, so a 2 GB clip takes about a minute.

What if a download stops halfway?

It resumes where it stopped, even for clips over 4 GB. Files already on your Mac are skipped, never replaced.

Does it run on Intel Macs?

Yes. It's a universal app for Apple silicon and Intel, on macOS 15 or later.

Does the webcam need Osmotic open?

No. In Webcam mode the camera is a standard USB webcam, so Zoom, Meet, FaceTime and OBS see it directly. The Webcam tab isn't tested on hardware yet.

Why does macOS say it can't verify the app?

This build isn't notarized by Apple yet. Open it once with System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway. Updates are signed, and their signature is checked before they install.

08Built on the work of others

Osmotic is a native macOS port of Osmosis by Konrad Iturbe, the Android app that reverse-engineered how DJI's Osmo cameras hand over their files on Wi-Fi. Camera control and the live view follow Kaze for DJI by Brian Merchant, with notes from OpenPocketCine.

Underneath it all are years of protocol research by the DJI OGs, dji-remote, osmo-download, DJI-Wifi-Connect, lib-osmo-ble, dji_protocol and reverse-engineering-dji, and the testers of Osmosis. Thank you.
