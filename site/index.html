Your Osmo camera,

01What it does

Tab: Files

The card, on your Mac

The Mac finds the camera over Bluetooth, joins its Wi-Fi and reads the card. Thumbnails by day, filters for videos, photos, starred and new. Download New takes everything that isn't on your Mac yet — into a folder per day, each file dated when it was shot.

Downloads resume if the link drops and never overwrite anything, even clips over 4 GB.

Tab: Live

Control it from across the room

Start and stop recording, take photos, switch between Video, Photo, Slow-mo and Low Light, and watch the camera's picture live — all over Wi-Fi. Back in Files, what you just shot is already listed.

Tab: Webcam

A webcam with a gimbal

Plug the camera in with USB-C and choose Webcam on it. The picture shows up in Osmotic — and in Zoom, Meet, FaceTime or OBS, even with Osmotic closed.

02And the details

  • Nothing twice

    A clip already on your Mac is skipped, never replaced. A half-downloaded one picks up where it stopped.

  • Light previews

    Space bar opens a preview from the camera's small .LRF proxy, not the full 4K file.

  • The extras

    RAW .DNG next to photos and the backup .WAV next to clips, if you want them.

  • Keyboard first

    Arrows to move, ⇧ to extend, space to preview, ⌘D and ⇧⌘D to download.

  • Updates itself

    New versions come from GitHub Releases and install only if their signature checks out.

  • Private by design

    No analytics, no accounts, no servers besides the camera. Nothing leaves your Mac.

03Install

  1. 01

    Download the .dmg, open it and drag Osmotic onto Applications.

  2. 02

    Open it. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the app — this build isn't notarized by Apple yet. Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

  3. 03

    Turn on the camera and click Connect. The first time, approve the pairing on the camera's screen.

While connected, the Mac uses the camera's Wi-Fi, so it has no Internet over Wi-Fi; Ethernet or an iPhone shared over USB keep you online. When you disconnect, your Mac goes back to your network.

05Built on the work of others

Osmotic is a native macOS port of Osmosis by Konrad Iturbe, the Android app that reverse-engineered how DJI's Osmo cameras hand their files over Wi-Fi. Camera control and live view follow Kaze for DJI by Brian Merchant, with notes from OpenPocketCine.

Underneath it all is years of protocol research by the DJI OGs, dji-remote, osmo-download, DJI-Wifi-Connect, lib-osmo-ble, dji_protocol and reverse-engineering-dji — and the testers of Osmosis. Thank you.
